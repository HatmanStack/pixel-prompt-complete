# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). It applies
to prompts you submit, images you upload, and images you generate.

On free, guest, and signed-out use, everything you generate is **published
publicly**. That raises the stakes on this policy: a violating prompt is not
private, it appears in a public feed we serve. Generations on a paid plan are
private, but this policy applies to them in full — privacy is not permission,
and we act on private content that violates the rules below.

## Not permitted

**Sexual content involving minors.** Any prompt or image that sexualises a
person under 18, real or generated, in any style including animation or
illustration. We report suspected child sexual abuse material to NCMEC as
required by law and terminate the account permanently. There is no warning and
no appeal.

**Non-consensual intimate imagery.** Sexual or nude depictions of an identifiable
real person who has not consented, including "undressing" or face-swapping a
real person into sexual content.

**Impersonation and deception.** Images of an identifiable real person
presented as a genuine photograph, fabricated events involving real people,
forged documents, currency, identification, or official seals, and content
designed to make a viewer believe something false happened.

**Harassment and hate.** Content that demeans, threatens, or dehumanises a
person or group based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion,
disability, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation, and content targeting
a specific private individual.

**Violence and self-harm.** Gratuitously violent or gory content, content
glorifying violence or a violent actor, and content that encourages self-harm,
suicide, or an eating disorder.

**Illegal activity.** Content facilitating a crime, including instructions or
depictions relating to weapons, explosives, or controlled substances.

**Infringement.** Content deliberately reproducing a third party's copyrighted
work, trademark, or brand in a way likely to confuse or infringe. Prompting by
a living artist's name to imitate their style is legally unsettled and we may
refuse it.

**Political and electoral deception.** Fabricated imagery of candidates,
officials, or electoral processes presented as genuine.

**Abuse of the Service itself.** Circumventing quotas, rate limits, or the
content filter; sharing accounts to defeat limits; automating access outside
any documented API; probing or attacking our infrastructure or our providers';
and reselling raw access to the underlying provider APIs. Building your own
product on top of the Service is fine; reselling the pipe is not.

## Provider policies also apply

Generation runs on four third-party APIs, each with its own policy. A prompt
this policy permits may still be refused by a provider, and repeated violations
can cost us access for everyone. The strictest applicable policy governs.

- [Google Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy)
- [OpenAI Usage policies](https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies/)
- [AWS Acceptable Use Policy](https://aws.amazon.com/aup/)
- [Adobe Generative AI User Guidelines](https://www.adobe.com/legal/licenses-terms/adobe-gen-ai-user-guidelines.html)

## How this is enforced

Prompts pass a keyword filter before generation, and each provider applies its
own moderation. **Both are imperfect in both directions.** The filter refuses
harmless prompts and permits harmful ones. That a prompt was not blocked is not
a judgment that it complies with this policy, and we may act on published
content after the fact.

Consequences scale with severity: a refused generation, then suspension, then
permanent termination. **Child sexual abuse material results in immediate
permanent termination and a report to NCMEC**, without warning.

We may remove any content for any reason, including content that violates
nothing but that we do not want to host. For public content that means removing
it from the gallery and feed; for private content it means removing it from
storage.
